## 1. The event that explains almost everything: 2026-10-22

On **Thursday, October 22, 2026** the AI/semiconductor complex sold off hard, and
the trigger was **Lam Research (LRCX)'s October 21 earnings call** — not the
individual prints of the names that fell.

Direct quotes from that call:
- CFO Doug Bettinger: *"this revenue guide would have been a bit higher if not for
  some near-term supply chain constraints that we are actively managing."*
- CEO Tim Archer: *"As we move into calendar year 2027, we see clean room space
  availability and supply chain constraints for certain components continuing to
  limit chip makers and equipment companies' ability to fully meet end demand."*
- Tim Archer: *"The whole industry is going to have to work through very similar
  things."*

  Philadelphia Semiconductor Index that day.

  GTC Berlin the same morning, described neocloud demand as *"off the charts."*

  free cash flow, Alphabet carrying historically negative free cash flow from AI
  buildout, and neocloud funding strain. CoreWeave (CRWV) fell 10% and sat about
  **39% below its early-October level**.
- Macro overlay: an attack on a Saudi oil facility pushed **Brent crude above
  $110 a barrel**, its highest since May, on inflation and borrowing-cost fears.
  Treasury yields fell as money went to bonds.

**Key analytical point available to every article:** on Oct 22 a name that had NOT
yet reported fell just as hard as one that had. AMD was down 8.10% with its print
five weeks away; VRT was down 7.81% with its print six days away. **Any single-day
move in AI names that week is contaminated by a sector event and cannot be read as

## 5. House rules for these rewrites

- 1,200–1,400 words. Do not pad. If a section has nothing sourced, cut it.
- No H1 at the top of the body.
- Meta description 70–160 characters.
- Use the phrase "pre-earnings" where it is genuinely accurate.
- Banned filler: "continues to be one of the most discussed topics", "this guide
  covers everything you need to know", "represents a meaningful opportunity", "let
  compound results work in your favor", "the landscape has shifted considerably".
- Vary sentence openings and structure. Nine of twelve recent articles were the
  same "X Pre-Earnings Setup" shape with heavy verbatim overlap — do not repeat
  that.
- `/wins` documents outcomes backward-looking. Never present it as predictive.
- Never write a number to satisfy the quality gate. The gate only counts numbers;
  it cannot check whether they are true.
