Pre-Earnings Trading Strategy for SNPS

Trading stocks before earnings requires understanding three key variables: the implied move (what the options market expects), the historical beat rate for the sector, and the current momentum leading into the report.

For SNPS, the next earnings report is scheduled for August 26, 2026.

The implied move is calculated using at-the-money straddles — if the straddle is priced at X%, the market is pricing in approximately a ±X% move in either direction around the earnings release.

  • Buy before earnings when the implied move is lower than historical actual moves — the options are cheap relative to what the stock has historically done.
  • Set a stop loss at 10% below entry for pre-earnings positions — earnings volatility can swing 20-30% on a miss.
  • Sell within 5 days of a beat — momentum after a positive earnings surprise typically fades within a week as short-term traders take profits.
  • Check short interest — high short interest (elevated) increases squeeze potential on a beat.
